# Send clips from your phone (PS5 and other phone clips)

OpenWorld Moments can receive clips from your phone over your home Wi-Fi. You
don't need a cable, a cloud service, or an app on the phone, just its browser.

## PS5 captures

1. **Get the clips onto your phone.** Turn on automatic uploads of captures
   from your PS5 to the PlayStation App, then download the clips you want to
   your phone from the PlayStation App. These menus are Sony's and can change;
   if they differ from this guide, follow PlayStation Support.
2. **Join the same network.** Put the computer and the phone on the same
   trusted network, such as your home Wi-Fi.
3. **Open the screen.** In OpenWorld Moments, choose **Import from phone**.
4. **Pick the network connection** your phone uses (usually your Wi-Fi), then
   choose **Show QR code**.
5. **Scan the code** with the phone's camera to open the upload page in the
   phone's browser. You can also type the address shown under the code.
6. **Send the clips.** Tap **Choose videos** and select them. The phone shows
   each file's progress, and the computer shows each one as it arrives and is
   added to the library.
7. **Finish.** When you're done, tap **I'm done** on the phone or **Close
   upload page** on the computer.

Clips sent this way are copied into the library, so you can delete them from
the phone afterwards. Sending the same clip twice is harmless: it shows as
"Already in the library".

## Staying safe

- **Use a network you trust.** The upload page is plain HTTP protected by a
  secret link. Anyone on the same network who gets the link could send files
  while the page is open. Don't use it on public, school, or hotel Wi-Fi.
- **The page doesn't stay open.** It closes after 10 minutes, when you leave
  the screen, when you tap **I'm done**, or when you quit the app. Each time
  you open it, the link is new.
- **It only accepts video.** It takes MP4, MOV, M4V, MKV, and WebM, up to
  10 GB per file. Anything else is refused and never added to the library.
- **The phone sees nothing of your library.** It can only send files.

## If something goes wrong

- **No network connection is listed:** connect the computer to your Wi-Fi or
  Ethernet, then reopen the screen.
- **The phone can't open the page:**
  - make sure both devices are on the same network;
  - some guest networks isolate devices from each other, so use your main
    network;
  - on Windows, allow the firewall prompt for private networks if it appears.
- **"This upload link is not active":** the page was closed or expired. Open
  **Import from phone** again for a new code.
- **A file says "Not added":** the file is damaged or isn't a video the app can
  read.
- **A file says "Interrupted":** the connection dropped. Choose it again.
